# ASCII Magic

The animated torus knot is adapted from [Torus Knot](https://www.ascii-magic.com/ascii/torus-knot) in [ascii-magic-art](https://github.com/kailashsr38/ascii-magic-art), source commit `b324cb0c60b38cadbf02659b1d3d9d6af2d0a4e1`. Rendered as 120 monochrome SVG frames in a 24-second loop, sampling one full 3D motion period (2π / 0.15 seconds). The daily profile generator embeds this checked-in artwork without executing third-party code.
